Card Surface — yakisugi (焼杉)
This card sits on the #2A2C32 elevated surface tone, framed by the #3A3D44 hairline border. The SVG noise overlay (4% opacity) applies globally across the viewport, giving the entire scene a subtle physical paper-fiber tooth. Behind it, the ink-mist layer uses mix-blend-mode: luminosity to extract calligraphic shading values and project them into the charcoal background.
