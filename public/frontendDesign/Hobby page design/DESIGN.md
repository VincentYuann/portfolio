## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the philosophy of *wabi-sabi*—the celebration of impermanence, restraint, and understated elegance—adapted for a modern digital portfolio. Designed for a multidisciplinary creator, the interface acts as an architectural canvas: hushed, deliberate, and deeply atmospheric. 

Drawing from architectural minimalism and Japanese tactile materiality, the interface evokes the sensation of nightfall in a Kyoto courtyard: deep *sumi* ink darkness, translucent *shoji* and *tatami* textures, crisp bone-white parchment typography, and sudden intentional strikes of sacred cinnabar (*vermilion*). 

The emotional signature is contemplative, commanding, and serene. It shuns the kinetic visual noise of generic web portfolios in favor of expansive negative space (*ma*), hairline-delicate framing, and tactile warmth.

## Colors

The palette draws its grounding and energy from natural mineral pigments and night light:

- **Primary Accent (`#C83C23` - Cinnabar Red / Torii Shūiro):** An iconic, consecrated lacquer red reserved for pivotal focal points: active indicators, status dots, subtle signature marks, and purposeful CTAs. Never overused.
- **Secondary Accent (`#E09443` - Moonlit Amber / Kohaku):** A warm, atmospheric amber light reminiscent of lantern-lit paper or moonlight filtering through courtyard screens. Used for subtle hover highlights, decorative metadata badges, and warm glows.
- **Tertiary (`#A7A39B` - Weathered Stone / Ishi):** A muted, mineral gray with warm undertones. Used for secondary prose, captions, timestamps, and resting state icons.
- **Base Background (`#18191D` - Deep Sumi Ink):** An immersive charcoal-black base that absorbs light, avoiding pure black (`#000000`) to retain organic depth.
- **Panel & Surface (`#24252A` / `#2A2C32` - Tatami Charcoal):** Layered surface tones providing tactile separation for elevated content containers, modals, and card surfaces.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#3A3D44` - Delimiting Ink / Kakejiku Stroke):** Hairline structure lines offering architectural discipline without harsh contrast.
- **Text Body & Headlines (`#E8E6DF` - Parchment White / Torinoko):** Soft, unbleached bone-white that delivers crisp contrast against the ink ground without retina fatigue.

## Typography

Typography balances classical editorial poise with contemporary technical readability. 

- **Display & Headings (Noto Serif):** Evoking Songti woodblock carvings and traditional brushed letterforms. Display and major headings remain in a regular weight (`400`) to maintain restraint and avoid aggressive heaviness. Generous line-height preserves breathing room.
- **Body & Functional UI (Manrope):** Geometric, modern, and exceptionally crisp at small sizes. Used for analytical project walkthroughs, metadata, UI controls, and labels.
- **Labeling & Metatags:** Applied with elevated letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) and transformed to uppercase when referencing project categories, dates, and navigation indices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is anchored in the Japanese aesthetic concept of *Ma* (間)—the conscious cultivation of silence, void, and interval. Negative space is treated as an active design material rather than empty canvas.

- **Grid Framework:** A 12-column asymmetric fluid grid structure on desktop that collapses to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. Maximum structural container width is restricted to 1280px to protect editorial legibility.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous vertical sections separated by `space-2xl` (80px), framed with subtle horizontal dividing rules (`#3A3D44`) spanning across grid margins.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (`< 768px`):** Single-column stacks, edge margin `1.25rem`, gutters `1.5rem`.
  - **Tablet (`768px - 1024px`):** 8-column layout, edge margin `2.5rem`, gutters `1.75rem`.
  - **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12-column composition with editorial offset columns, edge margin `5rem`, gutters `2.5rem`.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is tactile and grounded rather than glossy or synthetic.

- **Tonal Stratification:** Visual height is conveyed strictly through atmospheric ground layers rather than heavy drop shadows:
  - Ground / Void: `#18191D` (Canvas background)
  - Resting Layer / Panels: `#24252A` (Inset items, cards)
  - Elevated / Interactive: `#2A2C32` (Selected state, dropdowns, hovered cards)
- **Delicate Hairline Framework:** Cards and elevated sections rely on 1px borders using `#3A3D44`. Hover states shift the border subtly toward `#E09443` at 40% opacity or `#A7A39B`.
- **Moonlit Glow (Ambient Shadow):** Floating elements (such as pinned navigation bars or selected artwork preview modals) employ a diffused ambient shadow: `0 16px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 24px 0 rgba(224, 148, 67, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

In keeping with traditional joinery, sliding screens (*shoji*), and stone gardens, forms are disciplined, clean, and predominantly rectilinear.

- **Level 1 (Soft / Architectural):** Standard buttons, badges, card surfaces, and input boxes feature a minimal `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius. This softens technical harshness while remaining sharp and architectural.
- **Structural Lines:** Corner flourishes or borders never exceed 1px. Divider elements use quiet hairlines with intentional inset paddings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#C83C23` with `#E8E6DF` text. 4px roundedness. Hover introduces an inner moonlit glow or slight lift with background shifting to `#B5482E`.
- **Secondary (Wabi Outline):** Background transparent, 1px border `#3A3D44`, text `#E8E6DF`. On hover, border shifts to `#A7A39B` with a subtle background wash of `rgba(232, 230, 223, 0.04)`.
- **Ghost / Editorial Link:** Plain text in `#E8E6DF` with a hairline animated underline that expands from center or left in `#C83C23`.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- Background set to `#24252A` with a 1px `#3A3D44` frame.
- High aspect ratio photography / project imagery framed with fine inner padding (`space-md`), reminiscent of a mounted hanging scroll or framed print.
- Metadata (tags, year, discipline) set in `label-sm` with `#A7A39B`.

### Chips & Badges
- Compact height (24px to 28px), 4px radius.
- Dark charcoal fill (`#2A2C32`) with a fine border (`#3A3D44`).
- Accent chips feature a cinnabar or amber dot (4px circle) positioned left of the label text.

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Background `#1E1F24` with a 1px border of `#3A3D44`.
- Text color `#E8E6DF`, placeholder `#A7A39B` (dimmed to 50%).
- Focus state: Border transitions smoothly to `#C83C23` with an ultra-soft amber-red glow outline (`0 0 0 1px #C83C23`).

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- 16px square (checkbox) or circle (radio) with 1px border `#3A3D44` and surface `#18191D`.
- Checked state: Filled with `#C83C23`, carrying an off-white parchment checkmark or inner dot.

### Lists & Archival Tables
- Project index rows separated by full-width 1px hairline rules in `#3A3D44`.
- Interactive row hover transitions background smoothly to `#24252A`, with the title shifting from `#E8E6DF` to `#E09443`.
